# 4E4A1k3 watershed 3D viewer

**Live website: https://anantrajj7-sketch.github.io/watershed-4E4A1k3/**

Interactive 3D model of watershed 4E4A1k3 (2,363 ha, Pathardi and Ashti talukas, Maharashtra). It works on desktop and on phones, and the page is about 34 MB, so give it a moment to load.

## 3D water model

**https://anantrajj7-sketch.github.io/watershed-4E4A1k3/water.html** (about 10 MB; best on a computer with a graphics card)

An animation of rain, runoff, evapotranspiration, sideways flow and recharge, 2001 to 2025, with suggested conservation structures. Click a structure's dot to zoom to it; **Menus** (or M) shows the process table and view options. The figures are pyHELP estimates and are **not calibrated** against measurements. The structures are draft suggestions that have not been field-checked, their effect is not in the model, and the water and silt behind them are illustrations. Terrain heights are stretched 1.5 times. It uses the imagery, terrain and building sources credited below.

## How the page works
Scroll, hold the arrow keys, or press PageDown/PageUp (or Space) to glide between views (Home/End jump to the ends). Earth, India, Maharashtra (districts), Ahilyanagar (talukas) and the watershed lead into the interactive 3D model (drag to rotate). A satellite then crosses the watershed slowly, from one side to the other (it plays on its own after one PageDown), transmitting each data source it collects to a 3D knowledge-base book, which then closes into a corner icon. Ten soil profiles rise at different elevations, one is measured with a tape and sampled, the ten samples are laid out in a grid, and one is dissolved in a conical flask that turns beside a marquee of soil properties (depth, texture, pH, EC, CaCO3, N, P, K). The book expands, the list flies into it, and the final view zooms into the knowledge base. The intro map streams Esri World Imagery tiles and needs an internet connection; the outlines come from the supplied administrative boundary shapefiles.

## Data and credits
- Field parcels: Fields of The World (FTW Global, Taylor Geospatial; PRUE model on Sentinel-2, 2024 run), CC-BY, https://source.coop/ftw/global-data. 10 m pixel-based; boundaries were refined against Esri imagery.
- Buildings: GlobalBuildingAtlas (Zhu et al. 2025, ESSD 17, 6647), CC BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial use only.
- Imagery: Esri World Imagery. Source: Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community.
- Terrain: GEDTM30 30 m.
- Wells and groundwater levels: MRSAC and CGWB.
- Fields, trees and water are automatic estimates from imagery and have not been field-checked.
